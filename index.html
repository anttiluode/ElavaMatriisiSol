01 / Borrow to explore

New tips begin as an expense, not a profitable route. The patience control limits how long an unsuccessful shoot may keep its exploratory loan. Resource flows between neighboring nodes, not teleported to their tips.

02 / Flux reshapes conductance

Each connection responds to the resource it actually carries. Conductance rises with flux and decays otherwise. Compare with Fixed channels to see what changes without that feedback.

03 / No answer key

There is no global task score, backpropagation, or shortest-path oracle. Growth responds to nearby light, finite local reserves, and structural history. The sources supply energy; they are not labels.
