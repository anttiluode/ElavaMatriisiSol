What this establishes—and what it does not

This is a minimal signaling game, not a biological model of language. The two world factors and the existence of two signal slots are explicitly provided. The receiver learns through the harvest it actually obtains, and the sender learns from the returning economic receipt. No global cross-entropy, supervised target symbols, or backpropagation are used. Generalization to an unseen fourth combination shows the benefit of a pre-built compositional bias, not spontaneous grammar. The holistic baseline has the same four-message capacity but cannot recombine the two factors by design. Silence and shuffled-message tests check whether signal content matters. This is an independent signal-association experiment, not yet unrestricted vine growth or proof of semantic understanding.
