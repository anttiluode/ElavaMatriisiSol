Memories can remain stored even when the words are difficult to produce.

Intervene

Recognition reads stored associations in reverse; production also requires active accessibility. Both probes are defined by the model, not measured from humans.

Reading the experiment

Protected traces have low upkeep, but that upkeep is never zero. Extreme scarcity eventually destroys them too.

A positive savings result is expected when the rules explicitly preserve useful weights. The decisive controls are erased and scrambled associations, and a resource condition where protection fails.

Why this is not a claim about Swedish neurons

This is a constructive sufficiency test: six English meanings are paired with Swedish tokens, and the pairing is supplied during training. A slow association matrix retains structure, a fast accessibility gate decays through disuse, and maintaining dormant structure consumes a small resource budget. All of those mechanisms were chosen by the designer. A separate plant network would have to discover when to become dormant before we could call that emergent economy.

The experiment is inspired by research on foreign-language attrition and memory accessibility, not intended as a neurobiological reconstruction. Recognition and free production are different capabilities, and relearning can be faster than first learning even when production has lapsed. Here those differences have been translated into explicit, falsifiable mechanisms. See the methods, controls and ledger.
